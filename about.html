Vishal Car Rental & Tour and Travels is a Kota-based car rental and travel company. We started with a simple idea: renting a car should be as easy as sending a WhatsApp message. No long forms, no confusing fine print and no surprise charges when you return the keys.

Whether you are a student travelling with parents for coaching admissions, a family visiting Kota for a wedding, a tourist exploring Garadia Mahadev, the Chambal riverfront and Seven Wonders Park, or an executive on a business trip, we have a vehicle and a service that suits your journey.

Every car in our fleet is serviced, cleaned and sanitized before handover. We check tyres, brakes, oil, AC performance and documents so that you start your trip with confidence. Our team is reachable on call and WhatsApp throughout your rental, because a good trip is one where help is always one message away.

We believe in customer experience, and our service speaks for it. Rent your next self drive car with us and enjoy honest pricing, doorstep delivery and a team that actually picks up the phone.
